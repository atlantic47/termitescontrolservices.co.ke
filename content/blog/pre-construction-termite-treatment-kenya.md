# Pre Construction Termite Treatment in Kenya: Build Protected from Day One

Every building project in Kenya faces an invisible threat before the first brick is laid: subterranean termites living in the soil. Kenya's red laterite soils, clay-rich sub-soils, and deep loam profiles found across Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos, and the Coast region are home to some of the most aggressive termite colonies in East Africa. Without treatment at the construction stage, these colonies will find their way into the building's structural timber within years - or even months - of occupation.

Pre construction termite treatment is the most effective, most cost-efficient, and most permanent solution available for new buildings. It creates a complete chemical barrier in the soil before the foundation slab is poured - a barrier that termites simply cannot penetrate without picking up a lethal dose of termiticide. This guide covers everything architects, developers, contractors, and individual home builders in Kenya need to know.

## What Is Pre Construction Termite Treatment?

Pre construction termite treatment is the application of a registered termiticide to the soil at specific stages of a building project - before the structural concrete elements are cast. Unlike [post construction treatment](/blog/post-construction-termite-treatment-kenya) which must work around an existing structure, pre construction treatment is applied to exposed soil, meaning coverage is complete, access is unrestricted, and the chemical barrier can be placed exactly where it needs to be.

The treatment creates:
1. A **horizontal chemical barrier** beneath the floor slab - termites cannot rise through the soil into the building
2. A **vertical chemical barrier** around the foundation walls - termites cannot travel along the walls from soil to timber
3. A **perimeter barrier** around the building's exterior - the first and most important line of defence

This is why pre construction treatment is mandated under the **National Building Code of Kenya** for properties in termite-prone areas, and is routinely required by banks and mortgage institutions before they will fund residential construction projects.

## Why Pre Construction Is Far Superior to Post Construction Treatment

The comparison is clear:

| Factor | Pre Construction | Post Construction |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage completeness | 100% - all soil zones accessible | Partial - concrete obstructs access |
| Chemical efficacy | Maximum - full soil saturation | Reduced - injected under solid surfaces |
| Duration of protection | 5-10 years | 3-7 years |
| Cost per square metre | Low (open soil treatment) | Higher (drilling, labour, sealing) |
| Disruption to occupants | Zero - building not yet occupied | Moderate - drilling and chemicals in home |
| Risk of missing entry points | Very low | Moderate - hard-to-reach areas exist |

This is why architects and experienced developers always specify pre construction treatment in their project specifications. If your contractor is not including it, ask them why.

## When Is Pre Construction Termite Treatment Applied? (Exact Timing)

Timing is everything. Pre construction treatment must be applied at specific stages to be effective:

### Stage 1: After Excavation and Compaction, Before Blinding

Once the foundation trenches have been excavated and the formation level has been compacted, the first application of termiticide is made to the **bottom and sides of all excavations**. This treats the soil at the deepest level - the soil into which the foundation footings will be cast.

### Stage 2: After Backfilling Around Footings

Once the foundation footings are cast and the backfill soil is being placed around them, termiticide is applied to **each layer of compacted backfill** as it is placed. This creates a continuous treated zone in all the soil immediately surrounding the foundation structure.

### Stage 3: Immediately Before Blinding Concrete (Sub-Slab Treatment)

This is the most critical application. Immediately before the blinding (lean mix) concrete is poured under the ground floor slab, termiticide is applied at the specified label rate across the **entire formation level** - the complete footprint of the building. This creates the horizontal barrier beneath the slab. Read our detailed guide on [Termite Treatment Before the Slab](/blog/termite-treatment-before-slab) for the precise process.

### Stage 4: Around Plumbing and Service Penetrations

All points where water pipes, drainage pipes, electrical conduits, or other services penetrate the slab or foundation walls are treated individually. These penetrations are the most common entry points for termites into treated buildings, and careful treatment at this stage eliminates them.

## Chemicals Used in Pre Construction Termite Treatment in Kenya

Pestraid Kenya uses only Pest Control Products Board (PCPB)-registered termiticides for pre construction applications in Kenya:

**Fipronil (Termidor SC):** At a dilution rate of approximately 0.005-0.01% active ingredient, Fipronil creates a long-lasting non-repellent barrier in the soil. Its low active ingredient concentration means it is safe for the environment while being highly lethal to termites.

**Imidacloprid (Premise 200SC / Confidor):** Applied at label rates to the formation level, Imidacloprid provides 5+ years of termite barrier protection. Learn more in our guide on [Premise Termite Treatment](/blog/premise-termite-treatment-kenya).

**Chlorpyrifos-based formulations:** Still used in some pre construction applications in Kenya, though being phased out in favour of the newer non-repellent actives.

## What Does Pre Construction Termite Treatment Cost in Kenya?

Pre construction treatment is calculated per square metre of the building's ground floor footprint (the area of the slab to be treated). Typical rates in Kenya range from **KES 40-120 per square metre** depending on the chemical used, the number of application stages, and the location of the project.

For a 200m² ground floor slab, expect to budget **KES 8,000-24,000** for the complete pre construction treatment. This is a very minor line item against the total construction budget and one of the highest-return investments a builder can make.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is pre construction treatment required by law in Kenya?**
The National Building Code of Kenya specifies termite treatment requirements for new construction in high-risk zones. Many county governments and banks require a treatment certificate before approving building plans or disbursing construction finance.

**Can pre construction treatment be applied during the rainy season?**
Yes, but timing must be carefully managed. Treatment should be applied to soil that is moist but not waterlogged. Pestraid Kenya's technicians will assess site conditions on the day and advise on optimal application timing.

**What if my builder skipped pre construction treatment?**
It is not too late. [Post construction treatment](/services/post-construction-termite-treatment) can create an effective barrier around a completed building. However, it is more expensive, less complete in coverage, and has a shorter guaranteed lifespan than pre construction treatment applied before the slab.
