# Termidor Termite Treatment in Kenya: How It Works and Why It's the Gold Standard

When Kenyan homeowners and developers ask Pestraid Kenya which termite treatment chemical provides the best results, the answer is almost always Termidor. Termidor - whose active ingredient is Fipronil - is widely regarded as the gold standard in professional termite control globally, and it is registered for use in Kenya by the Pest Control Products Board (PCPB). Understanding how Termidor works, why it is so effective, and what to expect from a Termidor treatment can help you make an informed decision when comparing termite treatment options in Kenya.

## What Is Termidor?

Termidor is a professional-grade termiticide manufactured by BASF, formulated as a Suspension Concentrate (SC) containing 9.1% Fipronil as the active ingredient. It is specifically designed for use as a soil barrier termiticide - applied to the soil surrounding and beneath buildings to create a lethal zone through which termites cannot pass.

Termidor has been used commercially since the late 1990s and has accumulated an exceptional track record in Australia, the United States, South Africa, and East Africa - all regions with high subterranean termite pressure. In Kenya, it is distributed to licensed pest control operators and applied by certified technicians like those at Pestraid Kenya.

## The Science Behind Termidor: The Transfer Effect

What makes Termidor fundamentally different from older termiticides - and from every DIY treatment available in Kenya - is a mechanism called the **Transfer Effect**.

### How Older (Repellent) Termiticides Fail

Traditional repellent termiticides like Bifenthrin or Chlorpyrifos create a chemical zone that termites can smell and feel. When workers encounter the treated soil, they retreat and begin searching for an alternative pathway. In many Kenyan buildings, the alternative pathway exists through:
- Hairline cracks in the slab
- Service pipe penetrations that were not fully treated
- Areas where the barrier was not perfectly continuous

The colony survives, redirects its foraging, and continues to cause damage from a different entry point. The repellent barrier must be perfect - and in practice, it rarely is.

### How Termidor's Transfer Effect Changes Everything

Termidor is **non-repellent**. Termites that encounter Fipronil in the soil cannot detect its presence. Workers walk through the treated zone, pick up a dose of Fipronil on their cuticle (external body surface) and absorb it through ingestion of treated soil particles.

Crucially:
1. **The affected worker does not die immediately.** The slow-acting nature of Fipronil allows the worker to return to the colony before the lethal dose takes effect.
2. **The worker transfers Fipronil to nestmates** through grooming (workers groom each other as a hygienic behaviour, transferring chemical from cuticle to cuticle) and through trophallaxis (the sharing of liquid food between nestmates).
3. **The Fipronil spreads through the colony** - from workers to soldiers, from soldiers to reproductives, and ultimately to the king and queen.
4. **The entire colony is eliminated** - not just the foraging workers, but the queen, king, and all nursery castes. Without reproductive capacity, the colony collapses completely.

This colony elimination through the Transfer Effect is why Termidor delivers results that simply cannot be achieved with repellent termiticides or DIY products.

## Termidor Application in Kenya: How It Is Used

Pestraid Kenya applies Termidor in the following scenarios:

### Pre-Construction Soil Treatment

At the formation level (before the floor slab is poured), Termidor SC is diluted to approximately 0.005-0.01% Fipronil in water and applied to the formation level at 5 litres per square metre. This creates a long-lasting sub-slab barrier. Read the full process in [Termite Treatment Before the Slab](/blog/termite-treatment-before-slab).

### Post-Construction Perimeter Treatment

For existing buildings, Termidor is applied in the soil trench surrounding the foundation at 5 litres per linear metre of trench at 300mm depth, plus injection through drilled concrete at the same rate. ## How Long Does Termidor Last in Kenyan Soil?

Termidor's longevity in soil has been extensively studied. In tropical conditions similar to Kenya's:

- **Sandy / well-drained soils:** 5-7 years of effective residual
- **Clay-rich soils:** 6-10 years (clay holds Fipronil well)
- **High-irrigation sites (gardens, swimming pool surrounds):** 4-6 years due to higher water movement through soil

Pestraid Kenya's Termidor treatments come with a **5-year written warranty** with annual follow-up inspection visits.

## Is Termidor Safe in a Kenyan Home?

Yes. Termidor, when applied correctly by a licensed professional, is safe for humans, pets, and non-target wildlife. Key safety facts:

- **Mammalian toxicity:** Very low. Fipronil at field application concentrations in soil poses negligible risk to mammals. It acts on GABA-gated chloride channels in insects - a receptor type that exists differently in vertebrates.
- **Soil binding:** Fipronil binds strongly to soil particles and does not readily leach into groundwater at treatment depths (300-500mm below surface)
- **Re-entry:** Treated soil surfaces are safe for normal re-entry within 4-6 hours of application once dry
- **Garden plants:** No significant impact on established garden plants at label application rates
- **PCPB registration:** Termidor is fully registered with Kenya's Pest Control Products Board for termite control use

## What Results Should You Expect After a Termidor Treatment?

**Week 1-2:** No visible change. Termites continue foraging through the treated zone, picking up Fipronil doses.

**Week 2-4:** Transfer of Fipronil begins affecting the colony. Worker numbers start declining.

**Week 4-8:** Termite activity in the treated areas drops significantly. Mud tube construction ceases.

**Week 6-12:** Full colony elimination is typically achieved. A scheduled Pestraid Kenya follow-up visit at this stage confirms the result.

If any residual activity is observed at the follow-up visit, supplementary treatment is applied at no additional cost under the warranty.
