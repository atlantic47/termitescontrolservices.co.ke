# Termite Treatment Nairobi: Professional Solutions for Every Property Type

Nairobi is one of Africa's fastest-growing cities, and with that growth comes an expanding termite problem. The city's expanding suburbs — from Kitengela to Ruaka — are being built on soils that harbour massive subterranean termite colonies. Once a colony establishes itself beneath or within a building, it will feed continuously, 24 hours a day, seven days a week, silently destroying timber framing, flooring, furniture, and even the paper backing of plasterboard walls.

## Why Termite Treatment in Nairobi Requires a Professional Approach

Nairobi homeowners frequently attempt DIY termite control using diesel, used engine oil, kerosene, or over-the-counter pesticide sprays. These approaches share one critical failure: they kill surface termites while leaving the queen, the king, and the deep colony structure completely unaffected. Within weeks, the colony recovers, rebuilds, and resumes feeding.

Effective termite treatment in Nairobi requires:
- **Correct diagnosis** of species and infestation scope (see our guide on [signs of termite infestation in Kenya homes](/blog/signs-of-termite-infestation-in-kenya-homes))
- **Registered termiticides** approved for use in Kenya by the Pest Control Products Board (PCPB)
- **Professional application equipment** — high-pressure injection rods, soil trenching tools, and drilling equipment
- **A treatment warranty** backed by scheduled follow-up inspections

## Termite Treatment Methods Available in Nairobi

### 1. Soil Barrier Treatment (Termiticide Injection)

This is the most widely used termite treatment method in Nairobi for existing structures. It involves:

1. **Trenching** around the external perimeter of the building — a trench approximately 150–300mm wide and 300mm deep is cut adjacent to the foundation walls
2. **Treating the trench** with a registered non-repellent termiticide (such as Fipronil-based products or Imidacloprid-based solutions)
3. **Rodding** the treated soil at regular intervals to ensure uniform distribution of the termiticide at depth
4. **Drilling** through concrete paths, verandas, and internal floor slabs where trenching is not possible, injecting termiticide directly into the soil beneath
5. **Backfilling** the trench with treated soil

Non-repellent termiticides are the gold standard for this application because termites cannot detect them. Workers pass through the treated zone, pick up a lethal dose, and carry it back to the colony — including the queen — through a process called the **Transfer Effect**. This eventually eliminates the entire colony rather than just the foraging workers.

### 2. Pre-Construction Soil Treatment

For properties under construction, the most effective termite treatment in Nairobi is applied before any concrete is poured. This creates a **complete chemical barrier** in the soil that termites cannot penetrate for years. Read our detailed guide on [Pre-Construction Termite Treatment in Kenya](/blog/pre-construction-termite-treatment-kenya) for the full process and timing requirements.

### 3. Termite Baiting Systems

Where drilling or soil treatment is impractical — for example, in heritage buildings, fully tiled properties, or where treatment would disrupt a business — Pestraid Kenya deploys a **termite baiting network** around the perimeter. Monitoring stations installed in the soil are regularly checked; once termite activity is detected, they are loaded with a slow-acting bait that the workers carry back to the colony. Baiting is effective but typically takes longer to achieve colony elimination than direct soil barrier treatments.

For a detailed comparison, read [Termite Baiting Systems vs Soil Treatments](/blog/termite-baiting-systems-vs-soil-treatments).

### 4. Targeted Wood Treatment

For localized infestations in furniture, door frames, or window sashes, Pestraid Kenya applies a **borate-based wood preservative** or **deltamethrin-based contact treatment** directly to the affected timber. This is used as a supplementary method alongside soil treatment, not as a standalone solution for structural infestations.

### 5. Post-Construction Perimeter Treatment

For properties that have already been built without pre-construction treatment, a full [post-construction termite treatment](/services/post-construction-termite-treatment) creates the best available chemical barrier after the fact. This is the most common treatment type Pestraid Kenya carries out across Nairobi and is highly effective when applied correctly.

## Frequently Asked Questions: Termite Treatment in Nairobi

**How soon can treatment be applied after an inspection?**
In most cases, we can schedule treatment within 24–72 hours of a confirmed inspection report.

**Do I need to vacate my home during treatment?**
For soil barrier treatments involving drilling and injection, we recommend staying away from treated areas for 4–6 hours while the termiticide dries and odour dissipates. Most residents can remain in unaffected rooms. We will advise you specifically during the inspection.

**How do I know the treatment is working?**
Our technicians schedule a follow-up inspection 30–60 days after treatment. Termite activity in treated zones should have ceased. Any residual activity is addressed under the warranty at no extra charge.

**Will treatment damage my garden or plants?**
Registered non-repellent termiticides bind tightly to soil particles and pose minimal risk to established garden plants when applied by a licensed professional. We take care to avoid direct contact with vegetable gardens or water features.

**How long does a treatment take?**
A standard three-bedroom house typically takes 4–8 hours. Larger properties or those requiring extensive drilling may take a full day.
